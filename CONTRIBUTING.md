# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in Ultimate No Mercy!

## Reporting a bug or asking for a feature

Open an issue on this repository's **Issues** page and pick **Bug report** or **Feature request**. The form asks for what
we need: your version, the names of the ROMs in your `roms` folder, what happened, and how to make it happen again.

**Never upload ROMs, disc images, patches or any files from a game** -- name them instead.

## Code contributions

We're **not taking code contributions (pull requests) right now**: the project is changing quickly and is maintained by
its author. Bug reports and ideas are very welcome -- they are the best way to help.

## Updates

New versions are published on the **Releases** page. To be told about each one, click **Watch > Custom > Releases** at
the top of this repository.
